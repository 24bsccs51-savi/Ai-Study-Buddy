
This folder contains the initial ideas, problem identification, user needs, and preliminary research for the AI StudyBuddy project.

Project Idea

AI StudyBuddy is an AI-powered learning assistance platform designed to simplify the study process for students by using modern web technologies and Generative AI.

Problem Identification

Students often face difficulties while preparing for examinations because study materials are scattered across lecture notes, PDFs, textbooks, online articles, and handwritten notes.

The major problems identified are:

- Difficulty understanding lengthy study materials within limited time.
- Manual preparation of notes is time-consuming.
- Creating flashcards and revision questions is repetitive.
- Lack of personalized study plans.
- Difficulty assessing knowledge through self-generated quizzes.
- Switching between multiple applications for different study activities.
- Lack of a centralized platform for storing generated learning resources.

Proposed Idea

Develop a centralized AI-powered study platform that allows students to upload study materials and automatically generate:

- Concise summaries
- AI-generated flashcards
- Multiple-choice quizzes
- Personalized study plans

Initial Research

The proposed system uses Generative AI to process student-provided study content and create personalized educational resources.

The project is planned using:

- Node.js
- Express.js
- MongoDB
- Mongoose
- Google Gemini AI API
- JWT Authentication
- React.js

Expected Benefit

The system aims to reduce the manual effort required for preparing study resources and help students focus more on understanding and revising their academic content.
